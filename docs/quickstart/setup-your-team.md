# Setup your team

This page assumes your organisation has been set up and that you're an admin.

## Invite your team

1. Once logged in, you'll see your organisation name in the **top right corner**.
2. Click it to expand the additional options, including some limited to admins.
3. Click **Manage users** to invite your team.

From this page you can invite any team members who need access to the tool.

## Roles

elevaite365 currently supports two roles:

| Role | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| **Admin** | Full access, including managing users and organisation settings |
| **User** | Build, run and review tests |
