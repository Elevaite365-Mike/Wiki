Basic and Essentials support tiers, service levels, exclusions and fees.

## Preamble

These elevaite365 Support Services Terms ("Terms") govern elevaite365's provision of ongoing support for the elevaite365 software (the "Software") to every customer of elevaite365 ("Customer"). Basic Support applies automatically to every Customer as described in Section 3.2, without a separate signed order. Essentials Support applies only where Customer has executed a Support Services Order Form or other ordering document referencing these Terms (each, an "Order").

These Terms are incorporated by reference into, and form part of, the Consultancy Services Agreement between elevaite365 and Customer (the "Consultancy Services Agreement") and, where applicable, any Order. Capitalized terms used but not defined in these Terms have the meanings given to them in the Consultancy Services Agreement or the applicable Order.

Technical onboarding (Implementation) of the Software is a distinct, fixed-fee engagement governed by a separate statement of work and is not part of, or billed under, these Terms.

elevaite365 may update these Terms from time to time by posting a revised version at [support terms URL]. The version of these Terms in effect as of an Order's Effective Date governs for the remainder of that Order's then-current term; updates take effect for Customer at the start of its next renewal term under Section 9.1.

**Acceptance.** These Terms do not require a separate signature. They become binding on Customer upon execution of an Order that references these Terms, or upon Customer's purchase or use of Support Services, whichever occurs first.

## 1. Background and Commencement

**1.1** Implementation of the Software is scoped, delivered, and invoiced separately from these Terms, at a fixed rate agreed between the parties.

**1.2** Support Services commence on the earlier of: (a) the date of a documented Support Handover Meeting, at which the parties confirm Customer's environment, Basic Authorized Contact, and any additional matters relevant to support; or (b) the date that is sixty (60) days after Customer's Subscription Start Date (the "Outside Date"). If Support Services commence on the Outside Date without a completed Support Handover Meeting, elevaite365 and Customer shall use reasonable efforts to complete the Support Handover Meeting as soon as practicable thereafter, and these Terms apply as of the Outside Date regardless. This date is the "Support Services Effective Date."

**1.3** Before the Support Services Effective Date, any assistance provided by elevaite365 is delivered under the Implementation statement of work, not under these Terms.

## 2. Definitions

**2.1** "Order" means a Support Services Order Form or other ordering document under which Customer elects Essentials Support and which references these Terms. Basic Support does not require an Order.

**2.2** "Support Services" means elevaite365's provision of assistance to Authorized Contacts in response to discrete, ad hoc questions regarding the use of, and troubleshooting of, the Software encountered in Customer's ordinary use of the Software, provided in accordance with these Terms.

**2.3** "Authorized Contact(s)" means the individual(s) designated by Customer, up to the number permitted under Customer's selected Support Tier, who are trained on the Software and authorized to submit Tickets to elevaite365 on Customer's behalf. Customer's Basic Authorized Contact is identified at the Support Handover Meeting or by subsequent written notice; Customer's Essentials Authorized Contacts are identified in the Order.

**2.4** "Ticket" means a support request submitted by an Authorized Contact through elevaite365's support desk in accordance with Section 6.

**2.5** "Defect" means a reproducible error in the Software that causes it to fail to perform materially in accordance with its then-current documented specifications, as determined by elevaite365 in its reasonable discretion.

**2.6** "Enhancement" means any request for new functionality, or a material change to existing functionality, of the Software that does not constitute a Defect.

**2.7** "Training" means structured instruction in the use of the Software, including the onboarding of new users or refresher instruction for existing users, and is distinct from, and excluded from, Support Services as set out in Section 5.3.

**2.8** "Consultancy Rate(s)" means the hourly rates for the applicable personnel set forth in the Schedule of Rates in the Consultancy Services Agreement, as may be updated from time to time in accordance with that agreement.

**2.9** "Business Hours" means 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. in Customer's designated time zone, Monday through Friday, excluding elevaite365-recognized holidays. "Business Day" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday, or elevaite365-recognized holiday.

**2.10** "Subscription Start Date" means the commencement date of Customer's underlying software subscription with elevaite365, as set forth in Customer's software subscription or license agreement.

**2.11** "Certified Partner" means a reseller, implementation partner, or managed services provider that has obtained and maintains current certification from elevaite365 to deliver first-line support for the Software, in accordance with elevaite365's then-current certification requirements, which elevaite365 maintains and updates from time to time and makes available to Customer upon request.

## 3. Support Tiers

**3.1** There are two Support Tiers, summarized below. Basic Support applies automatically to every Customer as of the Support Services Effective Date and requires no Order. Essentials Support applies only once Customer has executed an Order as described in Section 3.4.

| | Basic | Essentials |

| **Included support hours** | None, all billable time is invoiced | 10 hours per calendar month |
| **Overage** | N/A | Billed monthly at the Reduced Rate (Section 3.3(c)) |
| **Minimum increment** | 30 minutes per Ticket, then 15-minute increments | 15-minute increments once Included Hours are exhausted |
| **Authorized Contacts** | 1 | Up to 3 |
| **Ticket volume** | Unlimited (billed per Ticket) | Unlimited (drawn from Included Hours, then overage) |
| **Service Level** | Section 7.4 (Basic Tier SLA) | Section 7.5 (Essentials Tier SLA) |

### 3.2 Basic Support (Time & Materials), applies automatically

(a) Basic Support applies automatically to Customer as of the Support Services Effective Date, without execution of an Order, for as long as Customer's underlying software subscription remains active.

(b) All Basic Support is billed at the applicable Consultancy Rate for the personnel performing the work, subject to the exclusions in Section 5.

(c) Time is billed with a minimum of thirty (30) minutes per Ticket, and in fifteen (15)-minute increments thereafter.

(d) Customer may designate one (1) Authorized Contact under Basic Support.

(e) Basic Support is governed by the Service Levels set out in Section 7.4.

### 3.3 Essentials Support (Monthly Subscription), requires an Order

(a) Customer pays the monthly Subscription Fee set forth in its Order, invoiced in advance in accordance with Section 8.2.

(b) The Subscription Fee includes ten (10) hours of Support Services per calendar month ("Included Hours"). There is no limit on the number of Tickets Customer may submit; Included Hours are drawn down only for time actually spent by elevaite365 responding to and resolving Tickets, subject to the exclusions in Section 5. Unused Included Hours do not carry over to any subsequent month and have no cash value.

(c) Time spent in a calendar month in excess of the Included Hours is billed monthly in arrears at ninety percent (90%) of the applicable Consultancy Rate (the "Reduced Rate").

(d) Customer may designate up to three (3) Authorized Contacts under Essentials Support, as identified in the Order.

(e) Essentials Support is governed by the enhanced Service Levels set out in Section 7.5.

### 3.4 Electing Essentials Support

(a) Customer may elect Essentials Support at any time by executing a Support Services Order Form specifying the Subscription Fee, Authorized Contacts, and requested start date.

(b) Essentials Support takes effect as of the first day of the calendar month following elevaite365's countersignature of the Order, unless the parties agree to a later date in the Order.

(c) Until Essentials Support takes effect under an executed Order, Customer remains on Basic Support.

## 4. Certified Partner First-Line Support

**4.1** Where Customer engages a Certified Partner, the Certified Partner may provide first-line support directly to Customer's users regarding use of the Software, in place of Customer's internal Authorized Contacts.

**4.2** Where a Certified Partner provides first-line support, the Certified Partner, rather than Customer's individual users, shall submit Tickets to elevaite365 for matters it cannot resolve, and the Service Levels in Section 7 shall run from elevaite365's receipt of the escalated Ticket from the Certified Partner.

**4.3** Use of a Certified Partner for first-line support does not change Customer's selected Support Tier, the associated fees, or the exclusions in Section 5, each of which continues to apply to Tickets escalated by the Certified Partner.

**4.4** elevaite365 maintains a current list of Certified Partners and the certification requirements for first-line support eligibility, available upon request.

## 5. Exclusions from Support Services

**5.1 Defects.** Time spent by elevaite365 confirming, troubleshooting, and remedying a confirmed Defect is not charged to Customer under either Support Tier and does not draw down Included Hours, provided that (a) the affected Software has not reached end-of-life or end-of-support, and (b) Customer's fees under any applicable Order, the Consultancy Services Agreement, and any applicable subscription or license agreement are current.

**5.2 Enhancements.** Time spent identifying that a request constitutes an Enhancement, rather than a Defect, is not charged to Customer and does not draw down Included Hours. elevaite365 does not perform custom development under these Terms. elevaite365 will log the Enhancement request and may, in its sole discretion, consider it for inclusion in the Software's product roadmap. Any decision whether to build an Enhancement, and the timing of any delivery, is at elevaite365's sole discretion; elevaite365 has no obligation to develop or deliver any Enhancement, and no Service Level in Section 7 applies to Enhancement requests. Enhancement requests are not billed to Customer and are outside the scope of these Terms.

**5.3 Training.** Support Services do not include Training. Customer is responsible for training its own personnel, including any new user, before designating that individual as an Authorized Contact, whether through a Certified Partner, a separately purchased elevaite365 training engagement, or Customer's own internal resources. If elevaite365 reasonably determines that a Ticket is, in substance, a Training request rather than a discrete support question, elevaite365 may decline to treat it as Support Services and will instead quote it separately at the applicable Consultancy Rate.

**5.4 Misuse and Unsupported Use.** Time spent on matters arising from Customer's failure to use the Software in accordance with elevaite365's instructions, from unauthorized modifications to the Software, or from issues unrelated to and not caused by the Software, is charged at the applicable Consultancy Rate (or drawn from Included Hours, as applicable) and is not treated as a Defect regardless of Support Tier.

## 6. Ticket Submission and Handling

**6.1** All support matters must be initiated by an Authorized Contact through elevaite365's support desk at [support portal URL], and must include a description of the issue, screenshots, and any other information reasonably necessary for elevaite365 to assess the matter.

**6.2** Upon receipt, elevaite365 will assign the Ticket a unique identifying number and make a reasonable assessment of its severity level in accordance with Section 7.

**6.3** Customer shall, at its own cost, provide elevaite365 with any remote access to Customer's environment reasonably necessary for elevaite365 to perform Support Services.

**6.4** elevaite365 may decline or suspend handling of a Ticket where it reasonably determines that: (a) the Software was not used in accordance with elevaite365's instructions; (b) after reasonable efforts, elevaite365 is unable to identify, reconstruct, or reproduce the reported issue; (c) unauthorized modifications or alterations have been made to the Software or software on which it depends; or (d) the matter does not relate to, and was not caused by, the Software.

## 7. Service Levels

**7.1** Support Services are provided only for supported versions of the Software that have not reached end-of-life or end-of-support, and only while Customer's fees under any applicable Order, the Consultancy Services Agreement, and any applicable subscription or license agreement are current.

**7.2** elevaite365 does not warrant that the Software will be available or free of Defects at all times. As part of Support Services, elevaite365 undertakes to use reasonable efforts to remedy Defects brought to its attention in accordance with Section 6, within the response times set out below, based on the severity level reasonably assigned by elevaite365's support desk.

**7.3** The response times below are measured from elevaite365's receipt of the Ticket and refer to an initial substantive response, that is acknowledgment, triage, and, where possible, a summary of findings and an estimate of resolution, and not to resolution itself. All times are targets and are approximate.

### 7.4 Basic Tier Service Levels

| Severity | Definition | Response time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Critical** | Incidents which significantly reduce functionality or performance of the Software, or which pose a significant security risk. | 12 Business Hours |
| **Major** | Incidents which cause, or are likely to cause, reduced functionality or performance of the Software, but which are not Critical. | 3 Business Days |
| **Minor** | Minor faults which do not affect Customer's daily use of the Software. | 10 Business Days |

### 7.5 Essentials Tier Service Levels

| Severity | Definition | Response time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Critical** | Incidents which significantly reduce functionality or performance of the Software, or which pose a significant security risk. | 4 Business Hours |
| **Major** | Incidents which cause, or are likely to cause, reduced functionality or performance of the Software, but which are not Critical. | 1 Business Day |
| **Minor** | Minor faults which do not affect Customer's daily use of the Software. | 5 Business Days |

**7.6** elevaite365 shall, after making all reasonable efforts to resolve a Ticket, have the right to close the Ticket where elevaite365 can demonstrate one of the conditions in Section 6.4, and will notify Customer of the basis for doing so.

## 8. Fees and Invoicing

**8.1** Basic Support fees are invoiced monthly in arrears at the Consultancy Rates, subject to the minimum billing increment in Section 3.2(b).

**8.2** The Essentials Subscription Fee is invoiced monthly in advance. Time in excess of the Included Hours is invoiced monthly in arrears at the Reduced Rate.

**8.3** All fees under an Order are subject to the payment terms set forth in the Consultancy Services Agreement.

**8.4** elevaite365 may adjust the Subscription Fee, Included Hours, Reduced Rate, or Consultancy Rates upon at least thirty (30) days' prior written notice to Customer, with any change effective as of Customer's next renewal term under Section 9.1.

## 9. Term, Tier Changes, and Termination

**9.1** Basic Support continues for as long as Customer's underlying software subscription remains active, on a month-to-month basis, without a fixed term or Order. An Order for Essentials Support has an initial term of twelve (12) months from its effective date, renewing automatically for successive twelve (12)-month terms unless either party gives the other at least thirty (30) days' written notice of non-renewal prior to the end of the then-current term, or unless the Order states a different term.

**9.2** Customer may elect Essentials Support at any time in accordance with Section 3.4, or downgrade from Essentials to Basic by written notice to elevaite365, with a downgrade taking effect as of the first day of the next calendar month.

**9.3** Either party may terminate Support Services (and any Order then in effect) upon thirty (30) days' written notice of the other party's uncured material breach, or immediately upon written notice if Customer's fees remain unpaid more than sixty (60) days past due.
