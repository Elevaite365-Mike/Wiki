# Partner Support Terms

Partner Enablement Support for support, implementation and WaveReady partners.

## Preamble

These elevaite365 Partner Support Terms ("Terms") apply to elevaite365 authorized Support, Implementation and WaveReady Partners, that is partners engaged to provide implementation, consulting, managed services, testing-as-a-service, or first-line support services to elevaite365's customers (each, a "Partner"). They govern the support elevaite365 provides to a Partner regarding Partner's own use and understanding of the elevaite365 software (the "Software"), and support for the consultants, managed service personnel, or testing-as-a-service personnel who are implementing, managing, or testing the Software on behalf of Partner's customers, during onboarding and pipeline development ("Partner Enablement Support").

These Terms are incorporated by reference into, and form part of, the partner or reseller agreement between elevaite365 and Partner (the "Partner Agreement"). These Terms are self-contained and address Partner's own support needs; they do not require Partner to consult any separate customer-facing agreement to understand its rights and obligations. The one exception is described in Section 1.2: where a customer has its own direct support arrangement with elevaite365, support for that customer is handled and billed under elevaite365's standard customer program, not under these Terms.

elevaite365 may update these Terms from time to time by posting a revised version at [partner support terms URL]. Updates apply to Partner as of its next Partner Agreement renewal.

**Acceptance.** These Terms do not require a separate signature beyond the Partner Agreement. They become binding on Partner upon execution of a Partner Agreement that references these Terms, or upon Partner's use of Partner Support, whichever occurs first.

## 1. Background

**1.1** These Terms apply to any partner that elevaite365 has authorized as a Support Implementation and WaveReady Partner, that is, a partner engaged to provide implementation, consulting, managed services, testing-as-a-service, or first-line support services to elevaite365's customers (a "Partner"), under a Partner Agreement. Authorization requirements are described in Section 2.2.

**1.2** These Terms do not apply where a customer has its own direct support subscription with elevaite365. In that case, if Partner (as an Authorized Partner) escalates a Ticket to elevaite365 on that customer's behalf, elevaite365 bills the escalation directly to that customer's own account under elevaite365's standard customer support program, not to Partner, and not under these Terms.

## 2. Definitions

**2.1** "Partner Effective Date" means the date Partner's Partner Agreement with elevaite365 takes effect, or the date of Partner's authorization, as specified in the Partner Agreement.

**2.2** "Authorized Partner" means a reseller, implementation partner, managed services provider, or testing-as-a-service provider that has obtained and maintains current authorization from elevaite365 as a Support, Implementation or WaveReady Partner, entitling it to deliver implementation, consulting, managed services, testing-as-a-service, and first-line support for the Software, in accordance with elevaite365's then-current training and authorization requirements, which elevaite365 maintains and updates from time to time and makes available to Partner upon request.

**2.3** "Partner Enablement Support" means elevaite365's provision of assistance to Partner personnel regarding Partner's own use and understanding of the Software, and support for the consultants, managed service personnel, or testing-as-a-service personnel who are implementing, managing, or testing the Software on behalf of Partner's customers, including in a demonstration, sandbox, or training environment, for the purpose of enabling Partner's consulting, implementation, managed services, or testing-as-a-service practice. Partner Enablement Support does not include any assistance in connection with Partner's development, testing, or support of Partner's own internal software product built using or incorporating the Software (a "Partner ISV Product"), as described in Section 2.8.

**2.4** "Partner Rate" means eighty-five percent (85%) of the applicable standard Consultancy Rate described in Section 2.7.

**2.5** "Enablement Period" means the period beginning on the Partner Effective Date and ending on the earlier of (a) the date twelve (12) months later, or (b) the date Partner has used twenty (20) cumulative hours of Partner Enablement Support (the "Enablement Hours").

**2.6** "Ticket" means a support request Partner submits to elevaite365's support desk regarding Partner Enablement Support.

**2.7** "Consultancy Rate(s)" means elevaite365's standard hourly rates for the applicable personnel, as set out in elevaite365's published Schedule of Rates and updated by elevaite365 from time to time.

**2.8** "Partner ISV Product" means any software product, application, add-on, or integration that Partner builds, develops, or offers using or incorporating the Software, other than Partner's provision of consulting, implementation, managed services, testing-as-a-service, or first-line support services to its own customers. Assistance related to a Partner ISV Product, including its development, testing, or ongoing support, is outside the scope of these Terms. If elevaite365 agrees to provide any such assistance, it is provided under a separate ISV or technology partner agreement, and is not free of charge, does not draw down Enablement Hours, and is not billed at the Partner Rate unless that separate agreement says otherwise.

**2.9** "Defect" means a reproducible error in the Software that causes it to fail to perform materially in accordance with its then-current documented specifications, as determined by elevaite365 in its reasonable discretion.

**2.10** "Enhancement" means any request for new functionality, or a material change to existing functionality, of the Software that does not constitute a Defect.

**2.11** "Training" means structured instruction in the use of the Software requested after the Partner's has completed their initial technical onboarding sessions, including refresher instruction, training additional/new personnel or training on new features, as distinct from the technical onboarding and discrete, ad hoc assistance provided under Partner Enablement Support.

**2.12** "Business Hours" means 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. in Partner's designated time zone, Monday through Friday, excluding elevaite365-recognized holidays. "Business Day" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday, or elevaite365-recognized holiday.

**2.13** "Partner Tier Program" means elevaite365's then-current partner tier framework, under which a Partner's commission rate and other partner benefits are determined by Partner's tier, based on sales performance metrics applicable to Partner's partner type (which may include, without limitation, deals closed, revenue or annual recurring revenue booked, or testing slots sold). elevaite365 maintains and updates the Partner Tier Program from time to time.

**2.14** "Program Year" means each successive twelve (12)-month period following the end of the Enablement Period.

## 3. Overview

**3.1** Partner Enablement Support is the support for Partner under these Terms. It covers both Partner's own product learning and the assistance elevaite365 provides in connection with Partner's delivery of implementation, consulting, managed services, testing-as-a-service, or first-line support to Partner's own customers.

| Attribute | Partner Enablement Support |
| --- | --- |
| **Purpose** | Partner's own product learning, plus support for Partner's consultants, managed service personnel, or testing-as-a-service personnel serving Partner's customers (excludes Partner ISV Products, Section 2.8) |
| **Cost** | Free during the Enablement Period (up to 20 hours / 12 months); thereafter, the Partner Rate unless adjusted by Partner's tier under the Partner Tier Program (Section 4.7) |
| **Billed to** | Partner |
| **Contact limit** | None |

## 4. Partner Enablement Support

**4.1** During the Enablement Period, Partner Enablement Support is provided to Partner at no charge, up to the Enablement Hours.

**4.2** There is no limit on the number of Partner personnel who may submit Tickets under these Terms; elevaite365 relies on the Enablement Hours cap, not a contact limit, to manage usage during the Enablement Period.

**4.3** elevaite365 will track Partner's cumulative use of Enablement Hours and will notify Partner as it approaches the twenty (20)-hour limit.

**4.4** After the Enablement Period ends, whether by elapsed time or by exhausting the Enablement Hours, Partner Enablement Support continues on a time-and-materials basis at the Partner Rate, invoiced monthly in arrears, subject to a thirty (30)-minute minimum increment per Ticket and fifteen (15)-minute increments thereafter, except as adjusted for a given Program Year under Section 4.7.

**4.5** Partner Enablement Support does not include, and may not be used for, any assistance related to a Partner ISV Product, as described in Section 2.8, regardless of whether Partner is within its Enablement Period or has remaining Enablement Hours.

**4.6** Where a Ticket relates to a specific customer that Partner is implementing, managing, testing, or otherwise supporting, Partner must confirm whether that customer has its own direct support relationship with elevaite365. If the customer does have such a relationship, the Ticket is instead handled under elevaite365's standard customer support program in accordance with Section 1.2, and is not Partner Enablement Support.

**4.7** For each Program Year, elevaite365 may grant Partner free Enablement Hours, a discounted Partner Rate, or both, based on Partner's tier under elevaite365's then-current Partner Tier Program for the preceding twelve (12) months. elevaite365 will notify Partner, in advance of each Program Year, of the free hours and/or rate applicable to Partner for that Program Year based on its tier. Any free hours granted for a Program Year apply only to that Program Year, do not carry over to a subsequent Program Year, and have no cash value.

## 5. Exclusions

**5.1** Time spent by elevaite365 confirming, troubleshooting, and remedying a confirmed Defect is not charged to Partner under these Terms, and does not draw down Enablement Hours, provided the affected Software has not reached end-of-life or end-of-support and Partner's fees under these Terms are current.

**5.2** Time spent identifying that a request constitutes an Enhancement, rather than a Defect, is not charged to Partner and does not draw down Enablement Hours. elevaite365 does not perform custom development under these Terms. elevaite365 will log the Enhancement request and may, in its sole discretion, consider it for inclusion in the Software's product roadmap; elevaite365 has no obligation to develop or deliver any Enhancement, and no particular response time applies to Enhancement requests.

## 6. Term and Termination

**6.1** Support under these Terms continues for as long as Partner remains an Authorized Partner in good standing under the Partner Agreement.

**6.2** elevaite365 may suspend or terminate support under these Terms if Partner's authorization lapses or is revoked, or if Partner's fees under these Terms remain unpaid more than sixty (60) days past due.

**7.1** These Terms are incorporated by reference into, and are governed by, the general terms and conditions of the Partner Agreement between elevaite365 and Partner. If these Terms conflict with the Partner Agreement on a partner-specific commercial term, the Partner Agreement controls.

**7.2** These Terms, together with the Partner Agreement, constitute the entire agreement between the parties with respect to Partner Support and supersede all prior discussions and agreements on that subject.
