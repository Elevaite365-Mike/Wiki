# Customer Essentials Support Order Form

*Customer agreement · Version 1.0 · Updated 4 Aug 2026*

!!! warning "Draft"
    This document is in draft and is not in force.

Used only to elect Essentials Support. Basic Support applies automatically.

Basic Support applies automatically to every elevaite365 customer as of the Support Services Effective Date, at no additional charge beyond standard Consultancy Rates, and does not require this form. This Support Services Order Form ("Order") is required only if Customer is electing Essentials Support, the paid monthly subscription tier described in the [elevaite365 Support Services Terms](customer-support-services-terms.md).

This Order is entered into by and between [elevaite365, Inc.] ("elevaite365") and [Customer Legal Name] ("Customer"). It incorporates by reference the elevaite365 Support Services Terms available at [support terms URL] (the "Terms"), and is subject to the Consultancy Services Agreement between the parties. Capitalized terms not defined in this Order have the meanings given to them in the Terms.

Essentials Support takes effect as of the first day of the calendar month following elevaite365's countersignature of this Order, unless a later date is specified below (Terms Section 3.4).

## What Essentials Support includes

- A flat monthly Subscription Fee covering ten (10) Support hours per calendar month, with no limit on the number of Tickets Customer may submit within that pool.
- Time beyond the Included Hours in a given month, billed monthly in arrears at the Reduced Rate, ninety percent (85%) of the standard Consultancy Rate.
- Up to three (3) Authorized Contacts able to submit Tickets, compared to one (1) under Basic Support.
- Faster response times than Basic Support, summarized below (Terms Section 7.5).
- Confirmed product Defects remain free of charge under Essentials, as under Basic, and do not draw down Included Hours.
- Training and Enhancement requests remain outside the scope of Support Services under either tier and are not included in the Subscription Fee (Terms Sections 5.2, 5.3).

| Severity | Definition | Response time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Critical** | Incidents which significantly reduce functionality or performance of the Software, or which pose a significant security risk. | 4 Business Hours |
| **Major** | Incidents which cause, or are likely to cause, reduced functionality or performance of the Software, but which are not Critical. | 1 Business Day |
| **Minor** | Minor faults which do not affect Customer's daily use of the Software. | 5 Business Days |
